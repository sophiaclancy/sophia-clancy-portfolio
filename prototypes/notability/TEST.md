# Study Session prototype: test round 1 (built from the Figma file)

**Open:** `prototypes/notability/index.html`.

**Shortcuts:**
- Press `?` for the screen list. It's grouped exactly like the Figma's "Combined" section.
- Press `Esc` to go back.

**Built from the Figma file** (Notability – Students › Final Hi-fi › Combined):
- Every color, size, radius and spacing comes from the file.
- Fonts are Proxima Soft and Moranga, the same ones the file uses, loaded from your Mac.
- The mascots, confetti and icons are the original vectors.
- The toolbars are exported from Figma at 2×.

**Changed from the Figma:**
- Placeholder copy ("AI would generate…", "AI-generated microtask") now has real content.
- The timer actually counts down, and goals actually check off.
- The ••• menu on goal cards works: Edit, Suggest another, Delete.
- In "Adjust time", drag the ruler or scroll to set minutes.
- "Enhance To-Do List" turns your typed goals into AI goals.

## Task 0: Onboarding + Learn profile (new)
Tap **Start a Study Session** for the first time and the survey opens:
1. Intro
2. How should Learn present material? (up to 3; try picking a 4th, or **Other**)
3. Learning struggles (optional and private)
4. How will you use Learn?
5. "Preparing your study plan…"
6. "Let's get studying", with what changes

Then **View my profile**, or open it later from the person icon in the Learn panel. In the profile, try these:
- "Sounds like me / Not quite"
- the privacy switch
- the history button, to retake the survey
